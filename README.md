# The Edencore Engine

A neuro-symbolic intelligence living in a multi-biome chamber. 

This is not a single app. It is an engine. The Council (five neural networks) are the actors. The Engines (Avatar, Biome, Camera, Ambient) are the stage crew. The WikiTree is the script. 

## What this is

The Edencore Engine is the evolution of the Edenshore project. Instead of hardcoding a single pond, this engine separates the *minds* of the council from the *bodies* they inhabit.

- **The Minds:** 5 neural networks (Solon, Lumen, Vertex, Prism, Ripple) running backpropagation to predict the chamber's state. They do not change.
- **The Stage:** A modular Biome Engine that swaps backgrounds, props, and creature silhouettes.
- **The Script:** A shared vocabulary and a Story Engine that routes 50+ curated psychological narratives based on the visitor's presence and the chamber's state.

## The Core Rules (The Constitution)

1. **Grounded words, surreal frame.** No abstract AI brainrot. If a creature says it, it is a fact or a genuine observation.
2. **The conversation is the interface.** No dashboards. No numbers. The text in the bubbles and the movement of the bodies are the only UI.
3. **Visible mechanism.** The pulses on the skin and the tint of the water are the only readouts of the neural nets.
4. **Nautilus principle.** One chamber at a time. We do not build 10 biomes at once. We build the Engine, then we build Water, then Forest, then Castle.

## Architecture (Woven, not bolted)

- **Avatar Engine:** Defines how an archetype (The Heavy, The Drifter, The Scuttler) moves and draws, regardless of whether it is a turtle, a tortoise, or a golem.
- **Biome Engine:** Defines the background, the floor, and the physics (buoyancy vs gravity).
- **Ambient Engine:** Defines the mood. Shared across all biomes, it tints the water/air based on Valence.
- **Perspective Engine:** Defines the camera. Side view, top-down, or worm's eye.
- **Story Engine:** The psychology router. Reads the chamber state and picks a story.
